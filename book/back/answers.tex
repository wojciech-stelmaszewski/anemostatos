\bookchapter{}{Answers}{The short answer to every exercise: the number to check, the sign to get right, the reason in a sentence.}
\label{app:answers}

\lead{These are answers, not solutions. Each gives the result an exercise asks for and, where it helps, the one step that leads there or the number the simulator measured. Look an answer up after you have one of your own: an agreement confirms it, and a disagreement says where to look again.}
