Generated by `make bench` (scripts/bench.ts → src/engine/arena.ts) on 2026-09-28.
Every controller flies every scenario on the same seeded conditions. Scenario columns: RMS 3D
position error in **cm** from the scenario's start of measurement. _Motor jitter_: RMS change of
the total thrust between 200 Hz samples, averaged over the scenarios — how hard the motors work.
_µs/step_: wall-clock cost per 1 ms physics step on the machine that ran it. Best per column in
bold.
